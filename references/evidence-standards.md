# Evidence Standards

Every operational claim needs a source, extraction time, reporting period, timezone,
metric definition, and relevant filters. Separate observed, calculated, assumed,
and unverified claims. Never fabricate customer quotes or missing data.

Use the company's authoritative metric definitions. Check denominator, grain,
attribution model, currency, deduplication, refunds, and delayed data before comparing
periods. Distinguish zero from missing. Reconcile conflicting sources explicitly.

Keep raw customer, employee, financial, and meeting data in approved private systems.
Share only the minimum necessary aggregate, with an access-controlled source link.
Synthetic examples must say they are synthetic.
