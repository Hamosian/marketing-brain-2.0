# Riverside Design System: Brand

## Logo

The Riverside logo ships in two component variants in the Figma library:

- White=False (dark logo, for light backgrounds)
- White=True (white logo, for dark backgrounds)

A combined Logo + Wordmark lockup is also provided as a vector.

Platform logos available as a Logo component (Platform axis): Riverside Light, Riverside Dark, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Facebook, Instagram, Linkedin, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Google Drive, Amazon, iHeart Radio, Deezer, Youtube.

## Core brand colors

- Primary brand: primary.c600 #9671ff (main), primary.c800 #7848ff (saturated)
- Primary surface (dark UI): secondary.c1000 #151515 background, secondary.c800 #222222 panels
- Upgrade accent: upgrade.c700 #c9f273 (neon green)

## Typography

Single typeface: Instrument Sans. Weights in use: Regular 400, Medium 500, Semi Bold 600, Bold 700. (Instrument Sans tops out at 700; the former Extra Bold 800 headings now use Bold 700.) See tokens/typography for the full scale.

## Background textures

The library includes branded background fills (image styles): Yellow Gradient, Violet Gradient, Yellow Haze, Violet Haze, Yellow Brushstrokes, Animal Print, Zigzag, Violet Brushstrokes, Geometric (Violet / Dark), Waves, Light Brushstrokes, Freehand, Abstract, Pastels, Fireflies, On Air (Light / Dark), Recording Icons (Dark / Light), Lavender, Violet. These are raster image fills in Figma and are not exported here as files; pull them from Figma when needed.
