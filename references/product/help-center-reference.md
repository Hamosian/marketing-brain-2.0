# Riverside Help Center - Complete Product Knowledge Reference

> Snapshot of the Riverside help center's structure and article set as of July 2026. Re-verify quarterly, since AI features and plan gating ship frequently. Sourced from `support.riverside.com` (plus a small number of third-party review sites, flagged inline where used). See "Caveats" at the end for what wasn't directly verifiable.

## TL;DR
- Riverside's help center is organized into **8 top-level categories** (Setting up your recording session; Recording equipment and setup guides; Recording in the studio; Editing your content; Hosting, sharing, and publishing; iOS/MacOS/Android app; Account, subscription, and billing; Riverside for Business), each with multiple sections containing overview, how-to, FAQ, troubleshooting, and policy articles.
- **Core product model:** Riverside records each participant *locally* in separate high-quality tracks (up to 4K/2160p video, 44.1 or 48 kHz audio), unaffected by internet lag, then offers an in-browser AI editing suite, live streaming, webinars, podcast hosting, transcription in 100+ languages, and a growing set of AI tools gated largely to Pro/Business plans.
- **Plans** range from Free (2 hours of separate-track downloads, watermark, up to 720p) through paid tiers (Pro, then the Grow and Webinar streaming tiers) to Business/Enterprise (SSO, SCIM, role-based team management, async recording, producer role, API). The affiliate program pays a **20% recurring commission** via Impact.com.

## Key Findings
- **Local separate-track recording is the defining architecture.** Riverside records from each person's own device first, so quality is not degraded by internet bandwidth; tracks upload to the cloud during/after the session. This is the central marketing differentiator versus meeting tools like Zoom/Meet.
- **AI is split into opt-in and built-in features.** Opt-in tools (Magic Audio, Find Fluff, Filler Words, AI Voice, VideoDub, AI Translation, AI B-roll, Eye Contact, Enhance video, AI Co-Creator, AI Chapters) must be actively applied by the user. Built-in features (Transcriptions and the "Made for You" suite: Magic Clips, Magic Segments, Magic Episodes, Hooks, AI Show Notes, Posts) are core to the platform, though Business users can disable some.
- **AI credits** power premium AI features: 1 credit = 1 minute of AI Translation, and 1 credit = 1 AI B-roll (5 seconds long). Available on Pro, Live, Webinar, and Business; purchased separately from the subscription; do not renew monthly; only account owners/admins can buy them. This feature is in beta and rolling out gradually.
- **Plan tiers gate** resolution, download hours, watermark removal, Magic Audio, the producer role, async recording, custom frame rates, higher bit rates, and enterprise features (SSO/SCIM/API).
- **Security posture:** Riverside is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and ISO/IEC 27001 certified (initially approved May 2022), uses encrypted connections and secure cloud storage, and aligns with GDPR/CCPA. Riverside publicly announced SOC 2 Type 2 compliance via PR Newswire on September 8, 2021; the report is available under NDA to Enterprise customers via security@riverside.fm.

## GLOSSARY (Riverside-specific terminology)
Definitions from the help center Glossary and related "About high quality tracks" article:
- **AI Voice** - Use text to generate an audio sample simulating your speaking voice; you can only create AI voice recordings with your own authenticated voice.
- **Aligned Track File (MP4/WAV)** - An uncompressed media file with empty space at the track's start to sync with other separate tracks when imported to editing software; aligned audio (WAV) is available when a studio records Audio Only.
- **All Participants Cloud Recording** - A backup file containing the audio and/or video of a session's recorded participants in one MP4.
- **Async Recording** - A participant records by themselves and shares the footage with the host afterward.
- **Audience Member** - Participant role that allows people to watch the recording session; not recorded unless granted permission during a Live Call-In.
- **Audiogram** - Turn an audio-only recording into a shareable video by adding visual elements (background, logo, captions, text, images).
- **High quality tracks** - Recorded locally on each participant's individual device, then uploaded to Riverside during or right after the session.
- **Local Recording** - A recording that takes place on the computer or device.
- **Media Board** - Studio feature where the host uploads and plays video and audio content during recording; the media is available as a separate track download after the session.
- **Multicam mode** - Use a mobile device as a second camera to capture an additional video angle during a studio session.
- **Noise Reduction** - When enabled while recording, lessens background noises (AC fans, heaters, ambient sounds) on the separated tracks of all participants.
- **Normalize audio levels** - On export from the editor, equalizes participants' audio levels into the same volume range.
- **Omnichat (Live stream chat)** - Integrates live messages from viewers watching on different channels into the Riverside chat, eliminating the need to monitor each platform separately.
- **Raw track vs. aligned track** - Raw is the participant's local track as recorded by their device; aligned adds start padding for synchronization.
- **Partial high quality track** - A high-quality track uploaded only partially (relevant when uploads don't fully complete).
