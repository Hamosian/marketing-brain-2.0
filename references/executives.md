# Executives - who Nir communicates upward to

Context on the people Growth Marketing has to influence: what they care about,
how they decide, what they repeatedly ask for, and how they want things
delivered. Load it when preparing anything that goes **up** - a 1-1 agenda, a
monthly or QBR narrative, a budget ask, a decision memo, a board slide.

Roster and reporting lines live in `references/team.md`, which stays the source
of truth for names, IDs and structure. This file carries only what is not
derivable from an org chart.

**Confidence rule.** Every line here is either observed (with a date) or stated
by Nir. Where a section is thin, it says so rather than filling the gap with a
plausible guess. Treat an uncaptured preference as unknown, not as "no
preference" - ask Nir instead of inferring.

## Abel Grünfeld - VP Marketing

Nir's manager. Reports to CEO Nadav Keyson. Slack `U01B8LQS944`,
`abel@riverside.com`. Owns the whole marketing department: Growth (Nir), Brand
(Raz Messing), Marketing/PMM (Sivan Mazuz), AI Marketing (John Tay), Growth
Initiatives (Ruben Aknin).

### How he wants things delivered

- **Laconic.** 1-1 agendas go to him as a numbered list of topic titles. No
  rationale, no sub-bullets, no "why this matters" clause. Established
  2026-08-02, reinforced 2026-08-09.
- **Slack DM, not a board.** Agendas and upward notes go to his DM and are never
  logged as tasks or ledger rows. The agenda closes when the meeting happens; it
  is not tracked work.
- **Suggestions go in chat, not in the artifact.** If there is a case for adding
  a topic, it is raised separately to Nir, never folded into the list sent to
  Abel.

### What he is working on directly

- **Trial-to-subscription CVR analysis and the web-decline RCA**, jointly with
  Sivan Mazuz (`systems/reference/marketing-operating-model.md`, Key People). Any
  Growth Marketing finding that touches trial conversion or web traffic decline
  is landing in territory he is already personally in - lead with what changed
  and what it means for that work.

### What to bring him

The Q2 priorities are the frame: content and brand awareness, PLG conversion and
activation, pipeline and revenue growth. A Growth Marketing update that does not
connect to one of the three is competing with four other sub-orgs for his
attention.

### Not yet captured

These would sharpen every upward deliverable and are worth Nir filling in when
convenient: how he prefers to be brought a decision (options with a
recommendation, or the raw trade-off), whether he wants bad news early or worked,
the questions he asks in every review, and what he consistently pushes back on.

## How to use this file

- **Preparing an Abel 1-1:** load this plus
  `.claude/skills/chief-of-staff/knowledge/1-1-agenda-style.md`. That file covers
  agendas for Nir's *leads*; the Abel rules above override it on format, because
  Abel's agendas are laconic topic titles rather than prepared action items.
- **Drafting anything upward:** apply the delivery preferences above, then run
  the content pipeline (`nik-voice` → `de-ai` → `critique`). An upward message is
  outward-facing.
- **Prioritizing Nir's day:** `/chief-of-staff` reads this to judge whether an
  item is exec-facing, which changes its lane.
- **Adding to it:** anything learned about how an executive decides or wants
  things delivered belongs here, dated, not in memory - it is team knowledge, not
  a personal preference (`CLAUDE.md`, Knowledge Routing).
