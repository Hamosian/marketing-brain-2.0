# LinkedIn guidelines for employees

Source: [Riverside LinkedIn guidelines for employees](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1X_1r0FYQzO9fxae1FnAiuSL5LPl8h-vMOW41SRq9YOk/edit) (Product Marketing team, under Sivan Mazuz). LinkedIn is one of Riverside's highest-leverage channels, and employee posts compound. Apply these rules to any LinkedIn content drafted for or by employees.

Questions, inspiration, or brainstorming: contact Charlee Perton (charlee.perton@riverside.fm).

## Tags and mentions

Do:
- Tag @Riverside (the [company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/riverside-fm/)), not @Riverside.fm, which is the website and will not link to the page.
- Tag teammates or collaborators when they are directly involved.
- Tag external partners, customers, or brands when it adds context or gives credit.

## Screenshots and visuals

Do:
- Share screenshots of the Riverside interface.
- Use clean, branded visuals or assets from the marketing team when available.
- Crop screenshots to what is relevant; no cluttered full-screen dumps.

Do not:
- Share screenshots of Google Meet, Zoom, or any competitor interface. It directly undermines the product.
- Post blurry, low-res, or unedited Riverside content.
- Include personal Slack conversations, internal dashboards, or anything confidential in visuals.
- Share anything with other people's faces, names, company, or content without their permission.

## Brand voice and messaging

Do:
- Lead with a point of view; dry announcements do not perform.
- Be human and specific. "I've been working on this for 3 months" beats "Excited to share".
- Align with Riverside's positioning (see `messaging-framework.md` and `riverside-story.md`).
- Use wit, humor, and personality. That is on-brand.

## Privacy and confidentiality

Do:
- Share only things you would be comfortable with anyone in the world seeing.

Do not:
- Share internal metrics, revenue figures, or roadmap details without permission.
- Post customer or business data, user information, or anything from internal tools (Notion, Slack, Linear, etc.) without permission.
- Screenshot internal conversations or share anything from a private Slack channel.
- Share information about unannounced partnerships, integrations, or product launches.

## Timing and coordination

Do:
- Coordinate with the marketing team on major launch moments; assets, copy, and messaging are often prepared.
- Post during the first 24 to 48 hours of a launch for maximum amplification, and comment on big Riverside launch posts within the first hour.
