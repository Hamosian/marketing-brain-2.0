# Riverside messaging framework

Source: [Messaging framework deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1zpe48lYcwfrS1b8B7hWc8z54xj8JYwQajqggbBz5vVE/edit) (Product Marketing team, under Sivan Mazuz). This is the canonical reference for how to describe Riverside, which benefits to lead with per audience, and which phrases are approved.

## Riverside in a nutshell

Approved for external use as-is.

**Short version:**

> Riverside is an end-to-end platform for creating studio-quality videos and podcasts, from recording and live streaming to editing, repurposing and distribution, all with the help of powerful AI.

**Long version:**

> Riverside is an end-to-end platform for creating professional videos and podcasts with full remote control and powerful AI, from recording and live streaming to editing and distribution. You'll get studio-quality results without the need for a studio, and a suite of AI tools to help you create better content, faster, and get more impact from each piece you record.

## The core insight

Humans have an innate drive to share compelling stories. Video is the primary medium for those stories, and the desire to tell them is often undermined by clunky tools, tech hurdles, and production chaos behind the scenes. Creators and teams do not want to wrestle with the "how" of getting content made. They want to focus on telling the story itself.

The problem framing is **the production maze**: too many stakeholders, too many tools, too much friction, too many delays. For companies, content stalls at multiple handoff points. For individuals, you are one person expected to use (and pay for) five different platforms.

## Core benefits

The internal framework below is a resource for creating external materials. Do not publish it verbatim.

### End-to-end creation platform

Key features: high quality remote recording (up to 4K), built-in text-based editor, HD live streaming to multiple destinations at once, webinars for up to 10,000 attendees (business only), AI content repurposing tools, podcast hosting and publishing.

Approved phrases:
- "End-to-end content creation platform"
- "Record, edit, and publish your content, all in one place"
- "From recording to publishing, all on one platform"

### Studio-quality results, no studio needed

Key features: local recording for high quality audio and video (up to 4K), Magic Audio (audio enhancement), audio level normalization, custom branding.

Approved phrases:
- "Create studio-quality content, no studio needed"
- "Studio-quality results, no studio required"
- "Local recording guarantees high quality audio and video, unaffected by internet connection"

### Speed

Key features: recording in separate tracks (editing flexibility: remove cross-talk, switch layouts, adjust audio per track, change aspect ratio), AI pre-edited episode draft and ready-to-share social clips ready when you finish recording, AI show notes, transcriptions and animated captions, built-in editor with AI tools (filler word, silence and fluff removal, audio enhancement, eye contact correction, AI translation and dubbing, smart layouts, correct and dub speech, B-roll generation), AI Co-Creator (AI agent that edits and repurposes content by chatting).

Approved phrases:
- "Hours of editing, done in seconds."
- "Save time on editing with AI tools that speed things up while keeping you fully in control"

### Maximum impact

Key features: Magic Clips (AI automatically creates ready-to-share clips from the recording's best moments), AI Co-Creator (repurposes a recording into clips, blog posts, thumbnails, social posts and more, instantly).

Approved phrases:
- "Get more out of every piece of content."
- "Turn every recording into an entire content drop."
- "Turn one recording into a week's worth of content."
- "The moment you finish recording, Co-Creator can turn your content into endless promotional assets, like clips, social media content, thumbnails, blog posts, and more."

### Ease of use

Key features: browser-based recording (no downloads for you or guests), instant assets made for you (full episode edits, social clips, transcriptions, show notes), text-based editor (edit video and audio like editing a doc), AI editing tools.

Approved phrases:
- "You don't need to be a video pro to make good video content"
- "You don't need to be an editing pro to edit professional content"
- "Easily edit video content just like editing a doc with the text-based editor"

### Total production control (business only)

Key features: Producer mode (join to handle technical needs without being recorded), adjust guest inputs and outputs (remote control over guest cameras and mics), lock brand kit for consistent branding across teams and projects.

Approved phrases:
- "Produce videos remotely with full control"
- "Full control over remote productions to avoid tech issues and ensure a professional, high-quality result"
- "Get full production control, from anywhere."

### Team collaboration (business only)

Key features: roles and permissions (share files inside or outside the company, organize teams by workspace, control project access), collaborative editing (comments and feedback directly in the editor), non-destructive timeline export to Adobe Premiere and Final Cut Pro, API access.

Approved phrase:
- "Work as a team with built-in collaboration tools that keep every project running smoothly."

## Per-ICP frameworks

Internal resource only. Each ICP has a key value proposition and three core benefits; match features to the benefit you are highlighting.

**For B2B and SLG targeting, Marketers is the main persona: marketing teams who produce their own content.** Producers are second, and only where video production genuinely sits under them. This ordering comes from the deck and it overrides any ranking derived from historical deal counts, which measure who we have sold to rather than who we target. The `riverside-intel` skill ranked Producers first and Marketing third until 2026-09-16; if you meet a copy that still does, it is stale and this file wins.

These frameworks are the Value Map side of a canvas (products and services, and the benefits that act as pain relievers and gain creators). The customer side, the ICP's ranked jobs, pains, and gains, is not recorded here; build it with `/value-proposition-canvas` from the voice-of-customer files in this directory and the demo-form pain map before writing for a segment you have not written for before.

### Producers

Key value prop: produce quality video and audio content fast, with full remote control, while collaborating with all stakeholders more efficiently.

1. **Unify your workflow**: high-quality recording, text-based multi-track editor, full HD live streaming, async recording (guests record on their own time via link), podcast hosting and distribution, repurposing, webinars up to 10,000 attendees, mobile app.
2. **Achieve studio quality, no studio needed**: local recording (up to 4K video, uncompressed WAV audio), producer mode, control guest inputs and outputs, Magic Audio.
3. **Accelerate content delivery**: team collaboration tools, separate tracks, progressive uploading (tracks upload during the session so editing can start immediately), timeline export and non-destructive editing (XML to Premiere and Final Cut), AI editing tools, custom branding with brand kit lock, AI translation, Co-Creator.

### Marketers

Key value prop: create high quality, on-brand videos at scale, faster and easier than ever.

1. **Unify your workflow**: same feature set as producers (recording, editor, live streaming, async recording, hosting, repurposing, webinars, mobile).
2. **Achieve studio quality, no studio needed**: local recording, Magic Audio, producer mode, control guest inputs and outputs.
3. **Scale content efficiently**: Co-Creator, separate tracks, Magic Clips, platform-ready layouts (one-click layouts optimized for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and more), AI translation, AI editing tools, custom branding, team collaboration tools (roles and permissions, collaborative editing, API, timeline export).

### Podcasters

Key value prop: end to end studio for easy, high quality podcast creation, from recording to distribution and promotion.

1. **Consolidate your workflow**: high-quality recording, text-based multi-track editor, full HD live streaming, podcast hosting and distribution, promotion and repurposing via Co-Creator.
2. **Achieve studio quality from your desk**: local recording and high quality audio and video, Magic Audio, mobile app.
3. **Save time with efficient post-production**: AI editing tools, instant assets (transcript, pre-edited full episodes and social clips on finish), AI show notes (summaries, key takeaways, chapters), progressive uploading, separate tracks, Co-Creator, custom branding (music, intros and outros, colors, logo).

### Solopreneurs

Key value prop: your end to end studio to create studio-quality promotional content fast, without the need for a studio or extra resources.

1. **Consolidate your workflow**: recording, editor, live streaming, hosting, repurposing and marketing content via Co-Creator.
2. **Achieve studio quality, no studio needed**: local recording, Magic Audio, mobile app.
3. **Create more content, faster**: Co-Creator, AI editing tools, instant assets, separate tracks, AI translation, custom branding via brand kit.

### Creators

Key value prop: your end-to-end studio to create high quality audio and video content, easier and faster than ever.

1. **Consolidate your workflow**: recording solo or with guests, editor, live streaming, hosting, promotion and repurposing.
2. **Achieve studio quality from your desk**: local recording, Magic Audio, mobile app.
3. **Create more content, faster**: AI editing tools, instant assets, platform-ready layouts, progressive uploading, Co-Creator, separate tracks, custom branding, AI translation.

## Tone of voice and brand personality

- **Personality:** clever, confident, warm, human.
- **Sound:** clear, witty, empowering, approachable.
- **Signature moves:** turn features into flexes, copy worth quoting, surprise with specificity, punch up never down, here to hype creators.
- **Essence:** the clever creative confidante our users want to hang out with. User first, always.

## How to apply this framework (worked examples from the deck)

- **Partnership pitch email:** use the "Riverside in a nutshell" blurb.
- **Landing page for producers:** use the producer ICP framework to guide the page story, matching features to each value highlighted.
- **Ad campaign on end-to-end for podcasters:** use the podcasters framework, end-to-end column.
- **Social post for self-serve users on speed:** general individuals framework, "speed and ease of use" benefit; use the features list for what to show and the "why should they care" values for resonance.
- **Newsletter sponsorship blurb for podcasters:** take the podcasters key value proposition as the base, with "Riverside in a nutshell" as a wording reference.
- **Event or publication blurb for marketers:** take the marketers key value proposition, format like the nutshell blurb.
