# Community voice: how users describe Riverside

Source: [Community voice doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ttyael4xvRcSVbCQgd_KhzmJh_mW9LjQcMs7QOzNdlw/edit) (Product Marketing team, under Sivan Mazuz). Around 90 community members answered "How would you describe Riverside to someone new?" This file carries the executive synthesis plus the most quotable verbatims. The full response set lives in the source doc.

Use this for voice-of-customer language in copy, social proof framing, and validating whether a proposed message matches how real users already talk.

## Executive takeaways

1. **Core identity is locked in: all-in-one production platform.** The single strongest, most repeated idea. Users say "all-in-one", "one-stop shop", "everything in one place", "end-to-end platform" unprompted. Riverside is not perceived as a feature tool but as the place where production happens start to finish.
2. **The product story users tell is a full production pipeline, not recording.** They describe record (remote, browser-based, local tracks), edit (text-based, "like a doc"), enhance (AI cleanup), clip and repurpose, publish, distribute and host. Recording is just the entry point.
3. **"Studio in the browser" is the dominant mental model.** "Online studio", "remote recording studio in your browser", "feels like having a team". One metaphor that explains quality, convenience, and remote collaboration at once. Stronger than "platform" in user language.
4. **Zoom is the default comparison, and Riverside is the better version.** "Zoom but better", "unlike Zoom, no glitches", "records locally". Zoom is the mental baseline users reach for when explaining Riverside to new people. Differentiation is consistently quality (local recording), professionalism, reliability.
5. **AI is not the core identity, it is a force multiplier.** Users say AI saves time, creates clips, cleans audio and video, makes solo creators feel like a team. They do not say "Riverside is an AI tool."
6. **The emotional payoff: professional output with low effort.** "Cuts my work in half", "makes me look like I have a team", "gamechanger". Core emotional equation: professional results plus simple execution equals empowerment.
7. **Riverside is expanding beyond podcasting in users' minds.** Video content, webinars, livestreaming, virtual conferences, YouTube, social. Podcasting is the entry point, not the boundary. The mental expansion: podcasting to content creation to media production platform.
8. **The real competitor is fragmented workflows, not features.** "I don't need multiple tools anymore." Users describe replacing 3 to 6 tools (Zoom, Streamyard, Buzzsprout, Otter, Descript, Audacity get named).
9. **Emerging theme: flexibility and scalability.** Flexible plans, "use what you need", "grow into features". Perceived as adaptable from beginner to advanced. Not dominant yet but strategically important.
10. **Community and support is a hidden trust layer.** When mentioned, it is about feeling supported while building something: helpful community, responsive human support.

## The distilled brand story in user language

> Riverside is an all-in-one browser-based production studio that lets creators record, edit, enhance, and publish high-quality audio and video content from anywhere, replacing fragmented tools like Zoom by offering a seamless, professional, AI-assisted workflow that helps individuals produce studio-level content and distribute it across platforms with ease.
