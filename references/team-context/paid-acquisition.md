# Paid Acquisition - team context

**Lead:** Raz Navon · **Function:** Paid Acquisition (ACQ) - Google/YouTube/Meta/LinkedIn/paid-LLM
**Source type:** `feed` team → `.claude/skills/growth-marketing-team-tasks/data/paid.md` (see `references/team-task-registry.md`)
**Last updated:** 2026-07-19 · ingested Apr / May / Jun monthlies + Jul weekly (the untitled "monthly_report_april_2026" doc is an ACQ report; no Feb/Mar report in the folder)

> Snapshot of insights, blockers, big bets, and performance from the team's own reports. Not live data - for current numbers route to `/rivermind:ask`; for source reports see the links at the bottom.

**Trajectory Feb→July:** Scaled aggressively through spring - April hit a record 37.9K generic signups on ~$1.44M spend, and generic MRR climbed from ~$38.5K (April) to a $45.7K all-time high in June while spend held near $1.4M. The through-line was volume growth outpacing trial conversion (generic SU→Trial slid from 11.5% to 9% in April), then a deliberate quality pivot: June delivered fewer signups but more subs and record MRR. A budget cut on Jun 22 (plus a second wave Jun 28) reset the baseline, and by early July the team is pacing at ~43-58% of targets with CPAs rising as campaigns re-stabilize. Strategic chips moved from raw scale toward YouTube geo-tested scale-ups, brand/product-launch pushes (Riverside 2.0), and new paid-LLM (OpenAI) channels.

## April 2026
- **Performance:** Record month - 37,874 generic signups, 3,420 trials. Generic First MRR $38,483 vs $47,085 target (81.7%); Brand MRR $40,281 vs $47,085 (85.5%). Total spend $1,436,892 (100.5% of budget). Generic Signup→Trial CVR fell from 11.5% (Mar) to 9.0% (-2.46pp). Brand SU→T held ~33%. YouTube/paid video spend $217,056 (+71% vs March).
- **Insights:** Signup volume scaled +56% vs March, mechanically dragging trial CVR down; largest drag was DSA + non-brand adding signups but few trials. Brand softened -17% vs March but held conversion - gap to target is volume-driven, not efficiency-driven. Editing GTM exploded (+173.7% trials); Clips high-volume but below-average funnel.
- **Blockers:** TopGEOs DSA Prospecting quality declining (trials not translating to purchases), budget reduced. US_B2C_Broad has questionable-intent traffic, held conservative until cohorts mature. Trial cost rose to €371 (+25%). UA Manager role open (5 in screening).
- **Big bets:** YouTube geo test; new "grow your audience" creative brief; webinar double-CTA + PLG CTA (webinars spending ~$100K); Brand Love campaign investigation (Meta "Ultrasound" creative, LinkedIn brand + Pretty Little Marketers webinar); newsletter ramp-up.

## May 2026
- **Performance:** Generic MRR $43,668, +10.9% MoM, at 99.2% of $44,041 target. Generic signups 33,880 (-10.7% MoM but +130.3% vs target); trials 3,322. Brand MRR $38,660 (-5.0% MoM, 87.8% of target). Total spend $1,421,391 (+1.5% MoM). Social spend +86% (newsletter/awareness). YouTube acquisition down ~20% MoM (geo testing). Generic SU→Trial flat at 7.6%; Brand SU→Trial dropped 26.1%→22.9%.
- **Insights:** Mix improving - signups declined but MRR grew, so quality up. Transcription the only intent to grow MoM (+9.4% SU, +10.9% trials); Podcasting still dominant (12,638 SU) but -5.9%; VideoClips -21%. us_b2c_broad_desktop scaled to $250,641 (+34.9%) with improving output. YT geo-test showed positive lift.
- **Blockers:** Brand SU→Trial decline (26.1%→22.9%) flagged to monitor. Brand signups far below target (56.3%). Creative pipeline stuck: "Video creation is broken," multiple video hooks in backlog; brand-system update and sonic logo On Hold/in progress. Two Home LP and Descript/CoCo tests waiting for maturity / need decision.
- **Big bets:** Webinar Repurpose-vs-Professional A/B tests (desktop + mobile, live May 12); scaling social/newsletter awareness; YouTube geo-test rollout; PPC creative refresh (testimonial-style, UGC hooks, chat-based-editing demos).

## June 2026
- **Performance:** ALL-TIME HIGH generic MRR $45,737 (+5.1% MoM), 92% of revised $50,000 target (raised from $44,569). Generic signups 30,164 (-11.1% MoM, cut-affected); trials 3,055; subs 1,513 (+3.0%). Brand MRR $41,236 (+6.6%), signups 7,424 (+15.7%), subs 1,371 (+2.2%). Total PPC spend $1,277,647 (-12.4% MoM): Paid Search -17.2%, Paid Video +14.2%, Social Brand -64%. Funnel stable (V→SU 21.5%→20.0%, SU→T 8.7%→8.8%).
- **Insights:** Quality signal - fewer signups but more subs and record MRR confirms no efficiency degradation, only volume reduction from the cut. YouTube US Podcasting the standout scale-up: spend +85% ($67.5K→$124.8K), signups +103%, $/SU down $38→$35. Direct attribution +24% (YouTube brand halo). Transcription +7% again. Won CRO tests: Descript search A/B, Home PPC layout, Pricing free-trial CTA, Google one-tap sign-in.
- **Blockers:** Jun 22 budget cut (~9 days / ~30% of month; daily run-rate $45K→$36K); OpenAI LLM cut -69% Jun 28. Full impact lands in July. Creative blocked: "Made for you: Webinar Screen Sharing" overdue (Jul 2), two Webinars brandformance assets "Need fix," B2B Webinar expansion waiting for motion. Webinar intent -14.9% MoM.
- **Big bets:** YouTube Geo-Test scale-up (the account's best-performing lever); MCP launch video; paid-LLM/OpenAI channel (paused but retained); testimonial ads (main focus); July run-rate planned at ~$900K new baseline.

## July 2026 (weekly - Week Ending Jul 9)
- **Performance:** Pacing well below target after cuts. Generic Subs projected ~1,137 vs 1,956 target (58%); Brand Subs ~1,206 vs 1,793 (67%). Generic signups projected 6,238 (-16% vs Jun); MoM subs projections Generic -26%, Brand -12%. Google Search signup CPA up +27.9% WoW to €40.74 (campaigns not yet adjusted to new budget). July projected spend ~$725K vs ~$1.54M budget (43%). Paid Search Generic CPA/SU rose $39.2→$53.7.
- **Insights:** Efficiency leaders: US_PMax_Podcasting_Desktop_New ($29.7/SU) and TopGEOs_B2C_Alpha_Mobile ($26.6/SU). SU→Trial actually improved on generic (+1.6pp to 10.6%) even as V→SU fell -4.1pp (16.0%) - data-immature 9-day window. All top campaigns down -55% to -88% MoM spend from cuts. OpenAI back online post-verification with better CPC/CPM.
- **Blockers:** Sustained budget cuts (Jun 21-22 + Jun 28) suppressing all rollover metrics. Opus LP underperforming badly in Clips campaign (0.60% CVR vs 1.53% generic LP). US_Generic_Webinar CPA $208 due to LP quality/dev QA backlog. YouTube Brand $0 spend despite $20K/mo budget (no campaigns running). Creative: PPC Meta screen-sharing overdue (Jun 4), Webinars brandformance needs fix.
- **Big bets:** Open decision - scale back up now vs let ROI fully recover first. LinkedIn Riverside 2.0 Product Launch (Nadav Keyson post: 28.9K impressions, 5.66% CTR, 1,638 clicks on $3K, outperforming all promoted content), extended to Meta + X. Next CRO step: test "Drop video link" CTA on Opus LP. UA Manager hire close - 7 active candidates across 3 stages.
