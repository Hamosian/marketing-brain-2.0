> Snapshot of insights, blockers, big bets, and performance from the team's own reports. Not live data - for current numbers route to `/rivermind:ask`; for the source report see the link at the bottom.

**Trajectory (as of March 2026):** Execution-and-close phase - February's pipeline converted into signed contracts, active content development, and early publishing across multiple pillars (SERP, B2B Experts, Germany localization, Students/EDU, New Editor, AI on X/LinkedIn). Committed spend is halfway to target and deliverables two-thirds published, but the recurring theme is that awareness plays (Podnews, top-of-funnel views) aren't yet converting to paid, and the expensive AI vertical needs more runway. Two new agency partnerships (REACH, CreatorBuzz) added structured pipeline coverage.

## March 2026
- **Performance:** Committed (signed) $252,773 of $500,610 target (50.49%); spent to date $223,554. Deliverables confirmed 150/194 planned (76%); published 129/194 (65%). Promo-code redemptions 372 in March (+8% MoM, up from Feb's 344; Jan peaked at 393). Program detail: SERP Campaign exceeded target (43 videos signed vs 40 planned, 31 published; YouTube Affiliate SERP +16% MoM to 237, Position-1 rankings +11% to 101); B2B Experts 56/34 deliverables signed; New Editor 13 confirmed vs 6 planned; Riverside Educators 4/4 signed; AI on X/LinkedIn 5/5 signed (2 posts live). Top creators by redemptions: Think Media (95, THINKMEDIA), Red11 (43), Pat Flynn (35, declining), AI Master (32). Best performers: Think Media 3 ad reads, 24.6K views, ~$0.40 CPV, 18.1% top-of-funnel (127 views → 23 signups); Grace Andrews LinkedIn 27.5K views at $0.11 CPV; Hasan first AI post 31K views/41 bookmarks; PSS Creative Media 8.3K views (vs 2K-4K benchmark).
- **Insights:** Pipeline moves faster when deals are pre-qualified - rejections (Nathan Latka on $2,500 terms; Shane Parrish declined a $28K counter on a $175K ask; Alex Cattoni declined $3,500 on $5,000) argue for a creator scoring system weighting product usage, ICP alignment, and deal-size flexibility. Agency-managed creators (Wishly, Spacestation, Fame.so, BNOC) respond slower but close better when the brief precedes the offer. AI is the most expensive niche and hard to scale - most AI creators only use Riverside for recording, not the AI tools, so it takes longer to surface in content. Niche creators (Red11, PodMahal, PSS) convert well despite small audiences - pipeline should hunt them. The 1-month-free offer structurally beats percentage discounts; YouTube consistently converts best. Webinar onboarding is more hands-on than expected. Podnews is a brand-awareness channel, not direct response.
- **Blockers:** Podnews title sponsorship (22 sends, ~33K daily subs, 43.3% open rate, 2.99% CTR) did not convert - 17 signups across 715 funnel views, zero paid across all four messaging themes. AI vertical needs more runway before ROI. Students/EDU slowed by a time-consuming new agency agreement template with legal; awaiting REACH creator list to approve/sign. New Editor: 2 of 5 Discovery YouTube creators want product testing before committing. Wishly Group creator Obaid slow and off-brief. CreatorBuzz awaiting final briefing approval. Pretty Little Marketer webinar pushed from April to next quarter. Dalit OOO in April (RiverTrack limited; her creator pipeline needs cover). CreatorsHub on hold. Paid conversions lagging generally (one-month-free codes mask downstream paid).
- **Big bets:** Editor Launch campaign for April 20 (13 deliverables, 10 launch-day repost creators on LinkedIn/X). Two new agency partnerships - REACH/Dylan Hueys student campaign (10 college YouTube creators, $2K/integration, 11 deliverables, live before end of April) and CreatorBuzz for expanded B2B creator sourcing. B2B Experts push - 12+ creators in negotiation at month-end (Todd Clouser, Elfried Samba, Chris Cunningham, Rachel Karten, Amanda Natividad, Jay Schwedelson, Scott D. Clary, et al.), $10K offer out to Scott D. Clary. Scaling the AI vertical (Ace, Edward FM, Felix signed Mar 29; three more in pipeline). **RiverTrack Creator Scoring tool** - composite 0-10 score by campaign goal (Awareness/Performance/SERP) that auto-flags "Do not renew" with reallocation. Germany localization (first published video; SERP DE). ManyChat partnership (joint workshop + webinar). Renewing Pat Flynn next quarter for dedicated YouTube.
