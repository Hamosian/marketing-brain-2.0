- **Ziff Davis (SWZD) commercial terms.** Appointment setting, billed **per meeting held**, not per qualified meeting. $750 per meeting, on an insertion order of 120 appointments. Each invoice covers meetings held in the **previous calendar month**, net 30 (confirmed by Simon Potton, SWZD Enterprise Account Director, 2026-08-13). The only quality gate in the deal is that no-shows are not charged. Contacts: Andrew Rourke (Senior Client Success Manager, delivery), Simon Potton (Enterprise Account Director, commercial), Charles Green (the SDR who books and joins the calls), Helen Bautista-Salazar (invoicing, `helenb@spiceworks.com`). Nir took ownership of the relationship on 2026-08-10 after Dor's departure; all invoices and comms route to him. **Consequence worth holding onto: a meeting with a prospect who records nothing and has no budget is still payable.** Tying spend to qualification needs a contract change, not a better brief.
- **What the 31 calls between 2026-07-01 and 2026-09-14 showed** (Gong transcripts plus the Pre-Op record on each, analysed 2026-09-15): 5 qualified, 15 weak, 11 unqualified. 22 had no recording programme that fits Riverside and 23 had no budget that financial year. 3 became opportunities, 1 closed. The five that converted all had a prospect who had written down what they record before the call. The qualification brief is the 16 Sept 2026 email thread "What we want asked before a meeting gets booked" (Nir to Andrew Rourke and Simon Potton); Simon folded the five questions into three, agreed to put the handover in the email body, and to frame calls as exploration. From 2026-09-19 every LHO is scored before the meeting and labelled after it by `/vendor-meeting-quality` (rubric, ledger and the outcome baseline live in that skill).

**Trajectory Feb→July:** The team peaked early - March delivered Impact.com's best-ever affiliate month ($84K revenue) and hit ~35-40% of PLG targets, then drifted into a deliberate "optimization stage" in April (conservative budget; SaaSmart/MVF told to cut spend). PLG attainment eroded through spring: First MRR attainment fell from 35% (Mar) → 46% (Apr) → ~28% (May) → ~24% (June), and by June the team was candidly "significantly behind" with no clear near-term fix. The through-line is a chronic Impact.com attribution gap (untagged partner traffic undercounting FmRR ~2.5x) plus a strategic pivot: chips moved off underperforming review-sites/vendors and onto the PartnerStack affiliate channel (live mid-June) and a maturing B2B/SLG motion (ZiffDavis + Pursuit → new SDR vendors MemoryBlue and L&D Collective, plus a 7Wonders channel-partner POC).

## March 2026
- **Performance:** First MRR $4,378 vs $11,294 target (35%); New Subs 164 vs 403 (40%); Total Signups 2,276 vs 6,500 (35%); SLG 40 leads vs ~60 SQL target (67%). Spend ~$85K of ~$170K budget (50%). Impact.com hit its highest-ever month at $84K total affiliate revenue.
- **Insights:** Impact.com's headline revenue didn't convert to First MRR (only ~$1.5K attributed) - heavy annual deals plus attribution gaps (~30% miss); Capital One ($23K) and Rakuten ($21K) led but suffered cookie-overlap and cashback cannibalization. Affiliate ecosystem otherwise had its best month (NachoNacho $4K, TakeMedia $3.7K). SaaSmart ($33K spend, 17 subs) and MVF ($15.5K, 7 subs) growing but cost-inefficient. Closed first-ever SLG deal ($1,283 from a ZiffDavis lead).
- **Blockers:** Impact.com attribution (subs tagged "unknown," need Yaniv to implement last-known-source logic); PartnerStack onboarding blocked on data-team integration; HubSpot lead-routing issues; weak lead qualification (only 2 deals from 52 leads); SmartReach.ai underperforming.
- **Big bets:** PartnerStack integration; Student Beans & Ryze onboarding; Ryze Beyond campaign; review-site experiments (TrustPilot, TrustRadius); vendor optimization of SaaSmart/MVF/Capterra.

## April 2026
- **Performance:** First MRR $3,991 vs $8,722 target (46%, est. ~$11K adjusted); New Subs 140 vs 332 (42%); Total Signups 1,895 vs 4,939 (38%); SLG 17 SQLs vs ~18 target (94%). Slight MoM decline (SUs 1,895 vs 2,276; Trials 303 vs 332; Subs 140 vs 164). Total PLG spend $72,295.
- **Insights:** Decline was intentional - conservative budget plus April seasonality; SaaSmart and MVF asked to reduce spend while holding cost-effective CP-Trial/CP-Sub ("firmly in an optimization stage"). Rewardful the efficiency standout ($5.6 CP-Trial, 65 subs on $700). Capterra weak ($2,167 CP-Trial, 1 sub). Attribution gap persisted (~35-40%; adjusted Impact FmRR est. $6.8-8K). SLG conversion strong - ZiffDavis closed $1,283.
- **Blockers:** Impact.com attribution reconciliation pending (~2.5x historical adjustment); awaiting more leads from ZiffDavis and Pursuit before finalizing delivery agreements; Pursuit go/no-go deferred to May lead quality.
- **Big bets:** Five May pillars - finish PartnerStack onboarding & scale; dedicated review-sites budget (Capterra, G2, TrustPilot, TrustRadius) tracked independently; SLG double-down on ZiffDavis; vendor onboarding wave (TrustRadius, OMR, MemoryBlue; Ryze test); creator/affiliate program POC. Signed TrustPilot; negotiating TrustRadius, MemoryBlue, OMR.

## May 2026
- **Performance:** First MRR $3,406 vs ~$12,000 target (~28%, est. true $5.2-7.2K); New Subs 116 vs ~180 (~64%); Total Signups 1,861 vs ~3,500 (~53%). Total channel spend $99,321 (a monthly high, inflated by the one-time TrustPilot annual fee). B2B: 4 closed-won (all ZiffDavis), 5 new SQLs.
- **Insights:** Impact.com again the attribution story - Omni showed $1,189 FmRR (41 UTM subs) vs $59K raw platform revenue (annual values); true FmRR est. $3-5K, with Rakuten (211 actions/$17.7K), Takeads ($7.2K), Capital One ($7.9K), Red11Media ($4.2K) mostly untagged. Rewardful again most efficient ($26 CP-Trial). MVF top signup volume (~501) but weak visit-to-trial; MVF/SaaSmart CP-Trial elevated ($510/$540). Capterra and SaaSworthy 0 subs. B2B closing rate low: 4 closed-won vs 42 closed-lost.
- **Blockers:** Ryze paused for LP redesign/funnel audit (conversion too low); Impact.com UTM tagging gap unresolved; Capterra/SaaSworthy need renegotiation or reallocation; B2B pipeline needs follow-up quality and ICP refinement.
- **Big bets:** PartnerStack soft launch completed (full public launch targeted June); MemoryBlue B2B cold-outreach activated; TrustPilot annual contract signed ($15,826 upfront, capability through May 2027); ZiffDavis + Pursuit reactivated with inclusion lists; webinar product positioning explored to lift MVF/SaaSmart conversion.

## June 2026
- **Performance:** First MRR $3,556 vs ~$15,000 target (~24%); New Subs 125 vs ~200 (~63%); B2B SQLs 15 vs 30 (~50%); New SERP 4 vs 3 (133%, the only beat). Total budget/cost $154,400 ($57K B2B, $94.4K PLG). B2B: 1 closed-won (Agile Mind, $1,155 MRR, 6/2); $3,200/mo open pipeline (Bond Brand Loyalty).
- **Insights:** Team explicitly "significantly behind" with no clear near-term way to close the PLG gap. Impact.com gap persists - Omni $989 FmRR (33 UTM subs) vs $57.7K platform-reported (conservative Omni figure used until verified with Daniel). Rewardful the efficiency leader ($14 CP-Trial, 47 subs on $1,000); SaaSmart/MVF expensive; Capterra 0 subs on $8,700. PartnerStack early traction strong on its own platform (450+ visits, 8 signups, 2 trials in week 1) but Omni tracked only 4 signups all month - a tagging/attribution gap.
- **Blockers:** PLG gap has no clear near-term fix; PartnerStack launched later than planned (commission/tracking overran) and its Omni attribution undercounts; mid-June pause in B2B named-account outreach cadence needs restoring; Impact.com attribution unverified.
- **Big bets:** Scaling PartnerStack activation & partner outreach (live, on marketplace, 60+ partners onboarded); two new B2B SDR vendors - MemoryBlue (SDR-as-a-service, high-volume named accounts) and L&D Collective (enterprise meeting-booking, higher-ACV); Core Partners outreach in negotiation (BuiltItFirst, Bullhorn, Goodpods, Podchaser); launching the 7Wonders Channel Partners Program POC (top July priority).
