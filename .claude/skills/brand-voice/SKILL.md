# Brand Voice

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Establish audience, purpose, channel, and desired action.
2. Load approved messaging and voice examples from the local company profile.
3. Choose the register: operational update, customer message, public content, or leadership note.
4. Lead with the point; preserve evidence, qualifications, and the writer's intent.
5. Avoid invented anecdotes, customer quotes, personal mannerisms, and unsupported superlatives.
6. Run `de-ai` then `critique` for external-facing prose.

Without an approved voice, use clear, direct language and label it a neutral draft.
Do not imitate an employee unless the human specifically provides authorized examples.
