# Product Knowledge

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/product/README.md` and the configured approved product source.
2. Find evidence for each requested capability, limitation, plan, and price.
3. Distinguish generally available, beta, roadmap, and unknown behavior.
4. Record source and verification date; check current availability before external claims.
5. Return verified answers and precise evidence gaps.

Never treat marketing examples, former-company features, or an old demo as product truth.
