Heads up: your edits (status, priority, ETA) save in this browser and survive reloads, but do not write back to the source ledger. When you've made changes, an amber bar appears down here at the bottom listing them; hit Copy for Claude there and paste the block to me. Once I apply it, those items clear automatically on your next visit - no reload ritual, no Reset. Server-saved editing comes with the app phase.

Daily refresh is safe: when the morning run republishes this page, your pending edits are kept, not wiped. Anything the ledger has caught up to is cleared as confirmed; anything still unsynced stays put and shows in the amber bar below.

Today / This week: the band at the top is the same tasks seen through their dates, not a second list - Today is anything open that is due or overdue, This week runs through Thursday. Drag cards to set your order; drag a card between the two rows and its ETA is rewritten to match. To pull a dateless task in, open its team tile and hit its Today or Wk preset. Your order syncs back the same way as every other edit.

To read: reading material, kept separate from your tasks. It fills from the links you park in your Slack self-DM (captured each morning) plus anything you add with + Add to read. Check one off to mark it read, or hit → make task if it needs follow-up - that opens a task on My tasks and clears it from the queue. It no longer clogs your task list as a P0 review.
