# Good Morning

1. Read the local company profile and identify the current person, timezone, and reporting window.
2. Check which integrations are explicitly enabled. Verify their account identities.
3. Fetch relevant active work, deadlines, and approved team updates. Use the configured
   task provider, not an assumed board. Exclude completed, archived, and deliberately parked work.
4. Highlight urgent unowned items, decisions, blockers, and overdue commitments.
5. Return a brief with priorities, supporting links, and concrete next actions.
6. State unavailable sources. No connection means unavailable, not zero tasks or no activity.

With no company setup, offer a draft brief from user-supplied notes and explain the missing sources.
Do not search other employers' workspaces. Do not send the brief unless authorized.
Private brief artifacts belong under ignored local storage.
