# Brand Guidelines

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/design-system/README.md` and the approved brand source.
2. Confirm current logo files, colors, typefaces, spacing, imagery rules, and accessibility needs.
3. Apply identity consistently without substituting a previous employer's assets.
4. Review contrast, legibility, responsive layout, and export fidelity.
5. When guidelines are missing, label the result as an unbranded draft.

Return the asset and the brand source used, with unresolved choices clearly stated.
