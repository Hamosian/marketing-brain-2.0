### Adi from Atera (paid video learnings)
- YouTube is 20% of total cost. Split the total budget Meta vs YouTube (SLG and PLG).
- Specific audience; frequency 2-5 per user; name the pain and exactly how we solve it.
- One person talking to themselves and answering works on camera.
- YouTube takes time to work, but once a video works it's very hard to beat. 1-2 productions a month.
- Hook = audience, brand, solution.
- **Creative process:** audience or use case + pain + what exactly we solve. Testing two messages matters more than testing hooks. The brief says what we want to say to which audience, then covers the features, simply. Agree the script with the writer, then storyboard, then the 3D artist.
- **By channel:** Meta is more authentic and simple (UGC). On YouTube UGC didn't work; it needs more structure. Shorts take a few weeks to take off.
- **Actions:** SQL title analysis; [Metadata](https://metadata.io/platform/audience-targeting/) for B2B on Meta plus LGFs; change YouTube creative every 2-3 weeks; sales ask "how did you hear of us"; add product questions to user interviews; train a GPT on Atera and monday ads and break them into structure.
- **Measurement:** view-through (Campaign Manager 360). Listen to sales calls, make sure AEs ask how the prospect heard of us, and put the answer in HubSpot.
