# Self-Serve Lead Scoring Model (v3)

> The model that scores every self-service signup, decides which cohort they fall into, and feeds quality signals to ad platforms and lifecycle automation. Lives entirely in HubSpot calculated properties + a custom-code action for the explanation field. PLG only - no sales involvement.

## Purpose

Riverside has roughly 4.9M self-service contacts in HubSpot. The PLG funnel converts a small fraction of them to paid; the rest churn out of the trial / freemium path. Without a quality signal, we cannot:

- tell ad platforms which signups are worth optimizing toward
- trigger the right lifecycle nurture per cohort
- measure what makes trial-to-paid conversion happen
- build effective lookalike audiences

The lead-scoring model assigns every contact a 0-100 composite score and a tier (High Quality / Mid Quality / Low Quality) automatically, in real time, based on firmographic fit and product behavior. The score is the input to all PLG optimization downstream.

## Scope

Self-service signups only. Riverside's PLG path: free trial / freemium → in-product engagement → self-service paid upgrade. No sales-assisted handoff. No SDR, BD, or AE involvement on these contacts.

Typical profile: individuals or small teams up to 50 seats, podcasting / content / interview use cases, paying via personal or small-team card.

## Architecture

```
+------------------+    +-------------------+
| ICP signals      |    | Behavior signals  |
| (firmographic)   |    | (product usage)   |
+--------+---------+    +---------+---------+
         |                        |
         v                        v
  self_serve_identity_score   ss_behavior_score
         (0-40)                  (0-60)
         |                        |
         +-----------+------------+
                     v
        self_serve_composite_score
                  (0-100)
        + abuse, behavior-gate, staleness overrides
                     |
                     v
            self_serve_lead_tier
        (High / Mid / Low Quality)
                     |
        +------------+-------------+
        v                          v
  quality_signup             selfserve_score_explanation
  (strong/mid quality/no)    (human-readable narrative)
        |
        v
  Meta CAPI / Google EC / TikTok Events API
  Lifecycle automation per tier
```

All four scoring properties are HubSpot calculated properties. The explanation is generated by a custom-code workflow action.

## Component reference

### `self_serve_identity_score` (ICP, 0-40)

Sums seven firmographic / role signals. Higher score = better fit for self-service paid conversion.

| Component | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Email type | 0-8 | `email_type` (Personal / Corporate / Educational) |
| Onboarding use type | 0-10 | `onboarding_questions_use_type` |
| Onboarding intent | 0-8 | `onboarding_questions_intent` |
| Marketing source | 0-3 | `hs_analytics_source` |
| Job title score | 0-6 (continuous) | `job_title_score_ml` from Snowflake/Hightouch |
| Company size | 0-3 | `company_size_contact` (Zoominfo) |
| Geography | 0-2 | `ip_country_code` |

Continuous scoring on the job-title component avoids hard cliffs.

### `ss_behavior_score` (Behavior, 0-60)

Sums six behavior buckets. Capped at 60 to match the composite math.

| Bucket | Weight | Signals |
|---|---|---|
| Recording quality | 0-15 | `has_meaningful_recording`, `recording_length_in_minutes` |
| Recording activity | 0-8 | `total_recordings_count`, `user_edited_a_recording_yes__no` |
| Recency | 0-12 | `last_7d_recording_time`, `pct_weekly_change_recorded_minutes` |
| Collaboration | 0-12 | `number_of_participants_in_last_recording`, `number_of_studios`, `count_self_serve_accounts` |
| Feature depth | 0-9 | `used_producer_mode`, `used_screenshare`, `used_magic_clips`, `used_mobile` |
| Marketing engagement | 0-4 | `hs_analytics_num_page_views` + `hs_analytics_num_visits` (combined) |

`license_activated` was removed in v3 due to suspected data leakage (it likely fires post-conversion).

> **This table is the design, not the behaviour.** Six of the thirteen product-behaviour inputs
> above are populated for zero contacts, and two more sit under 8%. Recording activity, Collaboration,
> and Feature depth all fire far below their stated ranges. Measured 2026-08-13 - see
> [Known failure: behaviour-score inputs are empty](#known-failure-behaviour-score-inputs-are-empty-for-every-contact).

### `self_serve_composite_score` (0-100)

`identity + behavior` with three demotion paths:

| Override | Trigger | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Abuse | `suspicious_seat_abuse_score >= 50` | 29 (locked at Low) |
| Behavior gate | `ss_behavior_score < 15` | 59 (locked at Mid) |
| Staleness | `last_7d_recording_time = 0` AND `total_recordings_count > 0` | 59 (locked at Mid) |

### `self_serve_lead_tier` (calculated enumeration)

| Tier | Composite range |
|---|---|
| High Quality | >= 75 |
| Mid Quality | 30 to 74 |
| Low Quality | < 30 |

Threshold raised from 60 to 75 in v3. The previous cutoff produced 177K HQ contacts (3.6% of base) which diluted the ad-platform signal (too many "positive" events of mixed quality, the algorithms learned the wrong audience).

> **These are the v3 design thresholds and they are no longer what the workflow applies.**
> `systems/owned/hubspot.md` records that tier workflow `1785077505` was changed on 2026-07-12 to
> **High >= 23, Mid 12 to 22.99, Low < 12**, because 75 and then 51 were both above the score's
> observed maximum and left High Quality permanently empty. The two docs have disagreed since.
> Treat the hubspot.md figures as the live ones and this table as the v3 design intent.
>
> The reason the thresholds had to be lowered is the subject of
> [Known failure: behaviour-score inputs are empty](#known-failure-behaviour-score-inputs-are-empty-for-every-contact):
> the composite cannot reach its designed range while half the behaviour inputs are empty. Lowering
> the cutoff rescaled the symptom and left the cause in place.
>
> **`self_serve_lead_tier` is also not a clean function of the current score.** In the warehouse
> mirror the tiers overlap completely: Low Quality runs 0 to 51, Mid 0 to 71, High 0 to 80 (measured
> 2026-08-13). Contacts keep whatever tier they were last assigned, so the field mixes the
> pre-2026-07-12 unreachable-threshold regime with the current one. Any tier-based cohort analysis
> has to split at 2026-07-12 or re-derive the tier from the score.
>
> Separately, `self_serve_composite_score` goes badly negative for a small population: 4,374 contacts
> below zero, reaching -143,285. Rare (0.06%) but large enough to wreck any average taken over the
> raw column. Filter to `>= 0` or use a median.

### `quality_signup` (calculated enumeration)

The flag fed to ad platforms for conversion optimization.

> **The internal name is `quality_signup`.** Its *label* is "HubSpot Quality sign-up", and earlier
> revisions of this doc wrote the internal name as `hubspot_quality_signup`, which does not exist.
> A query or workflow filter written against `hubspot_quality_signup` fails with
> `property does not exist`. Verified 2026-08-13.

| Value (stored) | Condition |
|---|---|
| `strong` | tier = High Quality |
| `mid quality` | tier = Mid Quality AND `ss_behavior_score >= 15` AND `last_7d_recording_time > 0` |
| `no` | everything else |
| `Unassigned` | not yet evaluated |

Two-tier output lets ad platforms run value-based bidding with separate values per cohort.

**Measured distribution (2026-08-13):**

| Value | Contacts |
|---|---|
| `no` | 5,227,073 |
| `Unassigned` | 1,423,159 |
| `strong` | 185,021 |
| `mid quality` | **5,488** |

The second tier has effectively collapsed. `mid quality` requires `ss_behavior_score >= 15`, and the
behaviour score cannot reach its designed range because six of its inputs are empty (see
[Known failure: behaviour-score inputs are empty](#known-failure-behaviour-score-inputs-are-empty-for-every-contact)).
So the two-tier value-based-bidding design is in practice a one-tier signal: 185,021 `strong` events
against 5,488 `mid quality`. Repairing the behaviour inputs will move this ratio sharply, which is
the change paid acquisition needs to size before it ships.

### `signup_composite_score`

Intended as a frozen snapshot of `self_serve_composite_score` at `createdate`, stamped once and never updated, for prospective model validation. **Broken: populated for 0 of 6.4M contacts.** The composite score is not yet computed at the instant a contact is created (behavior inputs sync in from Snowflake shortly after), so a copy that runs on `createdate` captures a null. Superseded by the 24-hour frozen score (see [24-hour frozen score](#24-hour-frozen-score-leakage-free-validation)). Diagnose and either repoint it to the delayed-copy pattern or retire it.

### `selfserve_score_explanation`

Human-readable narrative built by a HubSpot custom-code action. Includes:
- Composite + tier
- Driver line (which component is primary, with both percentages)
- Tier line (threshold context)
- Override flags (which caps fired and why)
- Diagnostic notes (zero scores, ceiling hits)

Source: HubSpot custom-code action `selfserve_score_explanation` builder.

## What we want to achieve

| Goal | Metric |
|---|---|
| Cleaner conversion signal to ad platforms | CPL on quality cohort drops 15-30% on Meta / Google after 30 days |
| Tier-aware lifecycle nurture | Open / click / upgrade rates differ by tier (proves the model is working) |
| Trial-to-paid lift | Mid Quality nurture path identifiable and improvable |
| No false positives | Abuse-flagged contacts cannot reach HQ; stale users decay automatically |
| Better lookalike seeding | Strong cohort produces higher-converting Meta / Google lookalikes than current "all signups" seed |

## What we can do with it

1. **Ad-platform CRO.** Send `quality_signup` as a soft conversion event to Meta CAPI, Google Enhanced Conversions, TikTok Events API. Use value-based bidding with $X (Strong LTV) and $Y (Quality LTV).
2. **Lifecycle marketing automation.** Different email and in-app sequences per tier. HQ gets upgrade-focused content, Mid gets engagement-uplift nurture, Low gets reactivation or churn-recovery flows.
3. **In-product upgrade prompts.** Prioritize upgrade banners, trial-extension offers, and feature unlocks toward HQ users in the app.
4. **Trial-to-paid analysis.** Join `signup_composite_score` to `plg_paid_self_serve_at` to measure which signal mix actually predicts conversion. Feeds back into reweighting the model.
5. **Lookalike audience seeding.** Export the Strong cohort to Meta / Google as a custom audience. Build lookalikes from converters, not from all signups.
6. **Pricing / offer personalization.** Test different upgrade prices, plan recommendations, or trial extensions per tier in the in-product paywall.
7. **Reactivation campaigns.** Low tier contacts with strong ICP score (firmographic fit but no engagement) get a different reactivation track than Low tier contacts with weak ICP (no fit, no behavior).

## Known failure: self-serve signups leaking into MQL / BD outreach

**This model is PLG-only by design - self-serve signups must never enter an MQL / BD-outreach
motion.** A lifecycle workflow is currently violating that boundary: it promotes raw product
signups to the `marketingqualifiedlead` lifecycle stage and stamps
`hs_lead_status = "BD: New/Not Contacted"`, dropping self-serve users into BD's outbound queue
regardless of their self-serve tier. This is the single biggest source of false MQLs.

**Canonical case (2026-07-09):** contact record `234337455378` (email redacted - PII stays out of the
repo; look it up in HubSpot by record ID). Created via the **Riverside.fm Backend App** integration
(a product signup - `hs_object_source_label = INTEGRATION`), personal-domain email (`email_type =
Personal`), **zero form submissions** (`num_unique_conversion_events = 0`), and scored **Low Quality**
by this model (`self_serve_composite_score = 22`, `self_serve_lead_tier = Low Quality`,
`quality_signup = no`). Despite the model correctly saying "not sales-ready," a lifecycle workflow moved
the contact signup → Lead (`hs_v2_date_entered_lead` 23:26) → **MQL**
(`hs_v2_date_entered_marketingqualifiedlead` 23:27) within ~4 minutes of creation and set
`BD: New/Not Contacted`. No Pre-Op / demo was ever booked - this is a pure lifecycle-stage misfire, not
a Pre-Op MQL.

**Scale (verified 2026-07-14, portal 9154210):** **2,129** contacts entered `marketingqualifiedlead`
with zero form submissions and a `Riverside.fm Backend App` source in the first two weeks of July -
product signups mass-promoted into an MQL/BD motion they should never touch, and the dominant share of
all zero-form MQLs in that window. (2,129 is the deduplicated day-by-day total; it reconciles with the
authoritative grouped count. A single capped `COUNT(*)` under-reports - see the boundary note below.)

```sql
-- Reproduce the cohort (HubSpot query_crm_data)
-- Upper bound is 2026-07-15 on purpose: per systems/owned/hubspot.md, BETWEEN date
-- boundaries evaluate at portal-timezone midnight and the end is exclusive, so
-- '...-07-15' is what captures all of 2026-07-14. query_crm_data also silently caps
-- rows per call, so page (e.g. day-by-day) and dedupe by hs_object_id for a full list.
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM CONTACT
WHERE lifecyclestage = 'marketingqualifiedlead'
  AND num_unique_conversion_events = '0'
  AND hs_object_source_detail_1 = 'Riverside.fm Backend App'
  AND hs_v2_date_entered_marketingqualifiedlead BETWEEN '2026-07-01' AND '2026-07-15';
```

**Why it matters:** BD reps waste cycles on gmail self-serve users who will convert (or not) through
the PLG paywall, the MQL metric is inflated with non-marketing-qualified product signups, and the
self-serve tier signal is overridden by a blunt lifecycle rule.

**The guard to implement (owner: Jonathan Galili, HubSpot infra).** Whatever lifecycle workflow moves
new contacts to `marketingqualifiedlead` + `BD: New/Not Contacted` must **exclude self-serve product
signups**. Add a suppression branch on enrollment so the promotion cannot fire when any of these hold:

- `hs_object_source_detail_1 = 'Riverside.fm Backend App'` (product-signup provenance - the primary,
  most reliable gate), or more broadly `hs_object_source_label = INTEGRATION` from the product; and/or
- the contact carries a self-serve score (`self_serve_lead_tier IS NOT NULL` /
  `self_serve_lead_tier = 'Low Quality'` / `quality_signup = 'no'`) - i.e. it is being handled by this
  model and therefore belongs in the PLG motion, not BD's. (`quality_signup` stored values are lowercase
  `no` / `mid quality` / `strong` - see `systems/owned/hubspot.md`.)

Self-serve signups should land (and stay) in `subscriber`, per `systems/owned/hubspot.md`; only a real
marketing-qualifying event (a demo/dashboard form submission, `force_generate_pre_opp`, etc. - see the
Pre-Op creation triggers in `preop-data-intelligence`) should lift a contact out of the PLG track.

**Remediation of the already-affected cohort:** the ~2,129 July product-signup contacts already sitting
in MQL + `BD: New/Not Contacted` should be reset out of the MQL/BD motion (back to `subscriber`, lead
status cleared) so BD stops working them. Do this as a one-time backfill list alongside the workflow
guard, not before it (or they re-promote).

> **Backward lifecycle moves are blocked by default.** Writing an earlier `lifecyclestage` (e.g.
> `subscriber` over `marketingqualifiedlead`) in a single update is silently ignored - HubSpot keeps
> the furthest-progressed stage. To move a contact back you must **clear `lifecyclestage` to empty
> first, then set the target stage** in a second write (verified 2026-07-14 on record `234337455378`).
> `hs_lead_status`, by contrast, clears fine in one write. Any backfill (workflow or API) must account
> for this two-step, or use the portal's "set lifecycle stage backwards" workflow setting.

**Status:** documented 2026-07-14 after contact record `234337455378` was flagged. Workflow guard +
cohort backfill pending on Jonathan (tracked on the Marketing Operations Tasks board, `6257866754`).

## Known failure: behaviour-score inputs are empty for every contact

**Six of the thirteen product-behaviour inputs to `ss_behavior_score` are populated for zero
contacts.** The score is computed and stored, so nothing errors and no dashboard looks broken, but
three of the six buckets cannot reach the weight the model assigns them.

Measured 2026-08-13 against a contact base of 6,837,620 (HubSpot portal 9154210). Fill rates were
read twice, once by CRM query and once from the property settings "Used in" panel, and agree.

| Bucket | Weight | Signal | Fill rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recording quality | 0-15 | `has_meaningful_recording` | 3.93% |
| Recording quality | 0-15 | `recording_length_in_minutes` (calculation) | 7.39% |
| Recording activity | 0-8 | `total_recordings_count` | **0%** |
| Recording activity | 0-8 | `user_edited_a_recording_yes__no` (calculation) | 100%, but suspect (see below) |
| Recency | 0-12 | `last_7d_recording_time` | 80.21% |
| Recency | 0-12 | `pct_weekly_change_recorded_minutes` | 0.76% |
| Collaboration | 0-12 | `number_of_participants_in_last_recording` | **0%** |
| Collaboration | 0-12 | `number_of_studios` | 80.17% |
| Collaboration | 0-12 | `count_self_serve_accounts` | 80.02% |
| Feature depth | 0-9 | `used_producer_mode` | **0%** |
| Feature depth | 0-9 | `used_screenshare` | **0%** |
| Feature depth | 0-9 | `used_magic_clips` | 80.21% |
| Feature depth | 0-9 | `used_mobile` | **0%** |
| Marketing engagement | 0-4 | `hs_analytics_num_page_views`, `hs_analytics_num_visits` | HubSpot-native, not measured |

### What this means

- **Recording activity (0-8) never fires.** `total_recordings_count` is empty for all 6.8M contacts.
- **Feature depth (0-9) runs on one of four signals.** Only `used_magic_clips` carries data.
- **Collaboration (0-12) loses its strongest signal.** `number_of_participants_in_last_recording` was
  added in v3 and described in the migration notes as the "strongest unused PLG signal". It is empty.
- **The staleness override can never fire.** Its trigger is
  `last_7d_recording_time = 0 AND total_recordings_count > 0`. The second condition is false for
  every contact, so no contact is ever capped at 59 for staleness.
- **Two calculations report 100% fill and should not be trusted at face value.**
  `user_edited_a_recording_yes__no` is 100% filled while `last_editing_recording_snapshot`, the
  plausible source, is 0% filled. A calculation that always resolves to the same value looks healthy
  and carries no signal. Verify the formula before counting it as a working input.

Because behaviour dominates the composite, this also means the standalone model assessment's AUC of
0.64 was produced by a score running on roughly half its designed inputs, and the `ss_behavior_score
>= 15` gate and the 75-point tier threshold were both calibrated against that reduced score.

### Fixing it changes ad-platform spend

Populating these inputs can only move behaviour scores upward, which enlarges the High Quality tier
and changes the mix `quality_signup` sends to Meta, Google, and TikTok for value-based
bidding. Thresholds have already been moved once because of this: 75 and then 51 both sat above the
score's observed maximum, and on 2026-07-12 the cutoff was dropped to High >= 23 to make the tier
reachable at all. That rescaled the symptom rather than fixing the inputs, and it means the current
thresholds are calibrated against a score running at roughly half strength.
**Do not backfill these fields and leave the thresholds untouched.**
Sequence it as: populate into shadow properties, re-measure the tier distribution, agree new
thresholds with paid acquisition, then cut over.

`total_recordings_count` has a ready source. The Community Milestones Hightouch sync
(monday item `12756362372`) produces a lifetime recordings count per account, and repointing the
existing empty property at it costs no property-limit headroom. The portal is at 972 of 1,000 custom
properties, so this matters.

## 24-hour frozen score (leakage-free validation)

Full config and validation guide: [Self-Serve Lead Tier Model: 24-Hour Frozen Score](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nEO3Hbtusxfsle66e_Wdkg-Mn2207a4cwOhS5lDqKWQ/edit) (HubSpot portal 9154210, internal). This section mirrors the substance; the doc holds the step-by-step HubSpot setup and the full custom-code action.

### Why

Every figure in the current model assessment uses the live score, which keeps moving with pre-purchase behavior. That makes the headline AUC of 0.64 optimistic: part of the apparent skill is the score reacting to activity close to the purchase. We need a frozen, leakage-free snapshot so the model's true predictive power can be measured prospectively.

`signup_composite_score` was meant to do this at `createdate` but is empty for all 6.4M contacts (the score is null at the instant of creation). A 24-hour delay fixes both problems: the score already exists by then, and the snapshot now includes first-day activation, which is the strongest signal we have (behavior dominates, and trailing 30-day recording time separated payers from non-payers by roughly 120x). A 24-hour freeze still lands before the pre-purchase ramp and before purchase for nearly all leads, so it stays leakage-free.

### Properties to create (Contact object, write-once)

| Property (internal name) | Field type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `composite_score_at_24h` | Number | Snapshot of `self_serve_composite_score` at 24h. Primary evaluation field. |
| `icp_score_at_24h` | Number | Snapshot of `self_serve_identity_score` at 24h. Component analysis. |
| `behavior_score_at_24h` | Number | Snapshot of `ss_behavior_score` at 24h. Component analysis. |
| `lead_tier_at_24h` | Dropdown | Snapshot of `self_serve_lead_tier` at 24h. Options match live tiers (High / Mid / Low / Unassigned). Tier calibration. |
| `score_24h_captured_at` | Date and time | QA timestamp set when the snapshot fires. Confirms the 24h timing after the fact. |
| `score_24h_pre_paid_flag` | Single checkbox | True when the contact paid self-serve within 24h of creation. Excluded from the evaluation to keep it leakage-free. |

Write-once is enforced by the workflow, not by HubSpot. Leave all fields editable by workflows with no default.

### Workflow

Contact-based workflow, `Score snapshot at 24h`. Enrollment: `Create date` is known AND `composite_score_at_24h` is unknown (so stamped contacts never re-enter). Re-enrollment off (the write-once guarantee). Delay 24 hours, then snapshot.

- **No-code (Option A):** copy-property-value actions for the four score/tier fields, set `score_24h_captured_at` to execution date, and an optional branch that sets `score_24h_pre_paid_flag` true if `plg_paid_self_serve_at` is earlier than `createdate + 24h`.
- **Custom-coded (Option B, Operations Hub Pro):** one Node.js action that reads the live values, guards against overwriting an existing snapshot, computes the pre-paid flag, and writes all six fields at once. Recommended (more robust, stamps QA timestamp and flag in one place). Full script in the Google Doc.

**Sync-timing caveat:** behavior inputs sync from Snowflake via Hightouch. If that sync runs once daily at a fixed time, the effective behavior window is 0 to 48 hours depending on creation time. `score_24h_captured_at` lets you verify this after the fact. Extend the delay to 36 hours to guarantee at least one daily sync has landed.

### QA (run 48h after activation, then weekly)

```sql
-- Fill rate: snapshots captured in a creation window (numerator)
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM CONTACT
WHERE createdate BETWEEN '2026-06-16' AND '2026-06-22'
  AND composite_score_at_24h IS NOT NULL;

-- Total created in the same window (denominator)
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM CONTACT
WHERE createdate BETWEEN '2026-06-16' AND '2026-06-22';

-- Source readiness: of recently created contacts, how many already carry a
-- live composite score. Confirms the score exists well before 24h.
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM CONTACT
WHERE createdate BETWEEN '2026-06-13' AND '2026-06-15'
  AND self_serve_composite_score IS NOT NULL;

-- Captured-but-zero check: a high count points to a sync-timing problem
-- (snapshot fired before first-day behavior synced in)
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM CONTACT
WHERE composite_score_at_24h IS NOT NULL
  AND composite_score_at_24h <= 0;
```

**What good looks like:** fill rate close to 100% of contacts older than 24h, `score_24h_captured_at` roughly 24 to 36 hours after `createdate`, and only a small share of captured snapshots at or below zero.

### Evaluation plan (leakage-free)

Once snapshots accrue and a fixed observation window has passed, repeat the standalone assessment's statistical battery on the frozen score.

- **Cohort:** contacts with `composite_score_at_24h` populated, created inside a defined window (e.g. all of July 2026), with `score_24h_pre_paid_flag` false.
- **Outcome:** first self-serve paid (`plg_paid_self_serve_at`) within a fixed horizon from creation (30 or 60 days), so every contact had equal time to convert. Wait out the full horizon before reading results.
- **Metrics:** AUC on the continuous frozen score and frozen tier (95% CI); conversion rate and lift by `lead_tier_at_24h`; cumulative gains and KS separation; standalone component AUCs for `icp_score_at_24h` and `behavior_score_at_24h`.
- **Headline comparison:** frozen AUC versus the current live-score 0.64. The gap is the size of the leakage. Expect the frozen number below 0.64 but above an at-signup, identity-only snapshot.

HubSpot SQL cannot express a not-paid condition directly, so non-converters at a threshold are derived as the whole cohort at that threshold minus converters at it. Run banded counts at thresholds 15 and 30 (and once with no threshold for totals) for each of `composite_score_at_24h`, `icp_score_at_24h`, and `behavior_score_at_24h`, plus two grouped tier-calibration queries (leads and converters per `lead_tier_at_24h`). Feed the bands into the Hanley-McNeil AUC + cumulative-gains script in the Google Doc.

**Sample size:** at a self-serve base rate near 0.24%, the CI is driven by converter count, not the millions of non-converters. Roughly 300 to 500 converters give a standard error around 0.02, tight enough to compare against 0.64. At typical creation volume, one monthly cohort plus the observation horizon usually clears that.

### Sequence

1. Create the six properties.
2. Build and activate the workflow (Option A or B). Re-enrollment off.
3. After 48h, run the QA queries; confirm fill rate and timing.
4. Diagnose and either fix or retire `signup_composite_score`.
5. Let one creation cohort accrue, then wait out the full observation horizon (30 or 60 days).
6. Run the banded counts and the AUC / gains computation; build calibration and component AUCs.
7. Compare the frozen AUC to 0.64 and report the leakage-adjusted predictive power.
