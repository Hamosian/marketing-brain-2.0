# Webflow (Vendor Contract & Billing)

> Webflow, Inc. as a paid vendor - contract, payment terms, and invoicing. Distinct from the Webflow *product* (the marketing site's CMS/hosting platform), which is covered in `systems/owned/marketing-website.md`. Finance (Ofer Levy / Haim Kallai) owns the contract and payment; Marketing is a requester/reader.

## Payment terms

**Net 30.** Confirmed in the June 2026 Order Form renewal thread - Haim Kallai (VP FP&A) explicitly required Webflow to "maintain the payment terms of Net 30 as it was," and Webflow's Eduardo Ibarra (Customer Experience) confirmed "the payment terms remain Net 30." No indication these terms have changed since.

## Contract flow (Order Form / DocuSign)

Webflow sends the Order Form via DocuSign (`webflow-contracts@webflow.com`, sender `dse_NA3@docusign.net`). Hanan forwards it to Ofer Levy (VP Finance) for signature; Legal (Rachel Efrat / Laura) reviews before sign-off. Renewals have gone through multiple void/resend cycles in-thread (e.g. June 2026) before the final signed version - don't assume the first Order Form email received is the final one.

## Invoicing / AP flow

Invoices arrive via `support@webflow-accounting.zendesk.com` (Webflow's AP/accounting ticketing). Payment-status questions route to `ap@riverside.com`, with Kseniya Pinsker as a fallback contact for follow-up if AP doesn't respond.

**Example (2026):** invoice 7807877-0113 ($46,070.95), due July 15, 2026 - 30 days after the June 15, 2026 signed Order Form, consistent with Net 30 - went past due and was confirmed paid via AP by July 28, 2026.
