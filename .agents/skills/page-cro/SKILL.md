# Page Cro

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Establish the target audience, traffic source, page type, and desired conversion.
2. Review the actual page on desktop and mobile; label inaccessible or untested states.
3. Evaluate message match, clarity, proof, objections, CTA, forms, and post-submit behavior.
4. Check accessibility and performance constraints that affect completion.
5. Use company analytics when available; do not invent conversion baselines or lift estimates.
6. Prioritize tests with a hypothesis, variant, metric, guardrail, and decision rule.

Return findings tied to page elements and a short experiment backlog.
