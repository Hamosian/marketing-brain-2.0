# Readers - who Nir writes to, and what each message is for

The register sets the format. This file sets the reader and the goal. Read the row for the reader before drafting, then write the reader brief (see `SKILL.md`, "Brief the reader first").

Built from Nir's own sent email and Slack, 2026-08-06 to 2026-10-04. Covers English writing only. For who someone is (role, reporting line, Slack ID) use `references/team.md`; for how an exec wants things delivered use `references/executives.md`. This file does not repeat either.

## How Nir's own writing looks

Constant across readers:

- **Opens with the point.** At most "Morning!" or "Hi,". Never a warm-up.
- **Numbers anything with two or more items.** When the reader numbered their questions, he answers in their numbering.
- **Asks for an ETA or an owner** more than anything else. "what's the ETA?", "can you...?", "is it a priority for you?"
- **Argues with money and data:** payback months, $ per lead, SQL counts, %.
- **Ships now, fixes later.** "I would rather go live with what we have right now and fix it on round 2."
- **Typography:** a spaced " - " as a joiner, `-->` for "leads to", dd.mm dates (15.10), uncontracted "would" ("I would rather").
- **Enthusiasm only for wins and people** ("amazing", "this is huge"). Never in an ask or a status.

## Polish by reader

Polish is the clearest sign that the agent wrote a message. Close readers spot it. Match the polish level of the row below. Never copy his typos.

| Level | What it looks like | Readers |
|---|---|---|
| **Fragments** | One line or a numbered list of short lines. Noun phrases are fine ("Attribution status."). No greeting, no closer. | Abel, direct reports, skip-level team, Sivan, Finance |
| **Plain sentences** | Short full sentences, one paragraph or a numbered list. "Hi," at most. | Sales and RevOps, Product and Eng, other Riverside teams, CEO and CRO |
| **Full email** | "Hi X," then paragraphs, numbered asks, signed "Nir". | Vendors, partners, prospects (prospects follow `inbound-demo-reply` PART 3) |

The tells to avoid at every level: `:wave:` openers, bold-led list items in a Slack DM, "Anything to add or cut, tell me here.", evenly paced paragraphs.

## The readers

| Reader | Shape | Usual goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Abel** | 1-8 words, or numbered answers in his numbering with dates. Commits to a date or says plainly it isn't feasible. Pushes back by asking for the constraint first. | Approval, a decision, protecting scope |
| **Erika** | Numbered questions that test the analysis, not verdicts. Short praise. Offers to unblock personally. | Test the analysis, get an ETA, push to ship |
| **Savion** | Short lines, one question each. Challenges the reasoning and coaches toward how it can work. | Decide, challenge, coach |
| **Raz Navon** | States the intent up front ("you don't need to answer, I'm just closing gaps"). Gives the frame: budget, definition of success. Never clipped: a context line, then "do we have the brief yet, and what's the ETA?" | Close loops, set the frame |
| **Hanan** | Bare noun phrases for status. HubSpot detail in plain sentences with links. | Status, data fixes |
| **Skip-level team** | @mention plus one question. Enforces process: Monday tickets, ETAs on the board. | Route work, enforce process |
| **Sivan and #marketing-management** | Questions priority before agreeing ("Is it a priority for you?"). | Prioritise, hold scope |
| **CEO and CRO** | Short and service-minded ("Sure. What info do you want to see?"). Updates are numbered wins with numbers. | Answer the ask, inform |
| **Sales and RevOps** | Data paragraph, then the rule of engagement, then a one-line go/no-go. Argues from the user's experience. | Route leads, enforce rules, flag the funnel |
| **Product and Eng** | "Hi," then numbered issues, each phrased as what the user experiences. Frames the test's purpose. | Fix what the user feels, protect the test |
| **Finance** | Ledger lines: the item, the figure, the owner. | Reconcile, assign the owner |
| **Internal specialists (legal, finance review)** | Questions inside their expertise, assertions only inside his. See `CLAUDE.md`. | Get their judgment |
| **A vendor he is correcting** | A blunt one-liner, or a memo that opens with the stat, carries the evidence and ends with numbered asks and a question back. | Hold them to the standard |
| **Partners** | Short negotiating turns, each ending on a question ("would that work?"). | Close terms |
| **Inbound prospects** | Owned by `inbound-demo-reply` PART 3. Never restate its rules here. | A booked demo |
