# Marketing Psychology

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Establish the audience's task, barriers, decision context, and desired outcome.
2. Select only relevant mechanisms, such as cognitive load, uncertainty reduction,
   credible social proof, loss aversion, or commitment.
3. Explain the mechanism and identify what evidence supports its use in this context.
4. Avoid deceptive scarcity, invented proof, hidden terms, or coercive interaction patterns.
5. Propose variants with a primary measure and user-experience guardrails.
6. Treat expected effects as hypotheses until tested.

Return a short diagnosis, concrete changes, and an experiment plan, not a list of biases.
