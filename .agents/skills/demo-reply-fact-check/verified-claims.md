# Verified Claims Ledger

Adjudicated product claims for prospect-facing copy. Written by `demo-reply-fact-check` Step 5, read by Step 2.

**Entries expire after 90 days**, and immediately on any packaging change. An expired entry is re-researched, not trusted. CONTRADICTED entries are the most valuable rows here: they stop a wrong claim being re-derived every week.

Format:

```
- **Claim:** <the claim, in plain product terms>
  **Verdict:** VERIFIED | UNVERIFIED | CONTRADICTED · **Source:** <file + section, or IKB article slug> · **Checked:** YYYY-MM-DD
  **Note:** <tier detail or why it failed>
```
