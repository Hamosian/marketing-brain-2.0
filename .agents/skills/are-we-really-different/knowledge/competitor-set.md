# Competitor set and pages to fetch

The roster this skill grades against. Segmentation follows
`references/messaging/riverside-story.md` ("Competitive positioning"), which
splits the market into production tools, creation tools, and growth platforms,
and names Riverside's edge as owning the creation step (the recording itself).

## Default set (use unless the user names others)

Four competitors. Enough coverage to catch category drift, few enough that the
run stays fast and the claim matching stays sharp.

| Competitor | Why it is in the default set | Pages to fetch |
|------------|------------------------------|----------------|
| Descript | The closest end-to-end claim in the market. Appears in both the production and creation groups in the brand story, so it is the sharpest test of our "one platform" claim | homepage, the AI or editing product page, pricing |
| StreamYard | Owns the live and streaming claim; the nearest rival on "studio-quality without a studio" | homepage, the studio or streaming product page, pricing |
| Zencastr | Nearest podcast-native rival; competes directly on local recording, which is our structural differentiator | homepage, the recording product page, pricing |
| Zoom | Not a category rival but the incumbent buyers compare against ("we already have Zoom"). Its claims set the floor for what counts as differentiated | the Zoom Sessions or webinar page, and the recording feature page |

Fetch the homepage plus the closest product page for every competitor. Pricing
is optional and only matters when the scope involves a plan or value claim.

Swap one competitor in when the scope calls for it. Say in the output that the
roster changed, so the score is not compared with a run that used a different
set.

## A note on the AI claim

`references/messaging/riverside-story.md` sets the principle: AI moved from **promise** ("we are an
AI-powered platform") to **proof** ("AI is what makes the change possible"), and
customers never lead with AI - they lead with time saved and output multiplied.

So any of our claims that leads with AI as an identity is almost certainly
**Drifted**, and should be flagged as such even when a competitor happens not to
use the same words. This is the one place where the category norm is strong
enough that the portability test understates the problem.
