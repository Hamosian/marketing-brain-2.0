# Value Proposition Canvas

## Context and Boundaries

Read `CLAUDE.md` and the configured local company profile. Missing company facts
remain unknown. Use only verified sources and authorized integrations for this company.
Keep private records and reports in ignored `local/` or approved company systems.
Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, spending, or changing live records.

## Workflow

1. Choose one segment and one buyer role; define the situation and business motion.
2. Build the Customer Profile: jobs, pains, and gains independent of the product.
3. Tag each item Assumed, Observed, or Verified with its source and date.
4. Build the Value Map: products, pain relievers, and gain creators.
5. Check fit between the ranked customer needs and actual product capabilities.
6. Turn the riskiest assumptions into Test Cards with method, metric, and threshold.
7. Capture a Learning Card after observing the result.

Return the profile, value map, evidence gaps, and next test.
Use only the new company's approved research; no interview quotations are preloaded.
